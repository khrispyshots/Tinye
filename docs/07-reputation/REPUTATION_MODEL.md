# Reputation model

**Status:** Draft | **Version:** 0.1 | **Last updated:** 2026-11-14  
**Responsible role:** Product / finance / engineering leads

## Purpose

Limit the initial feature to understandable savings history.

## Assumptions and dependencies

This is a documentation proposal, not an implemented or approved product. The master specification and owner approval govern scope. MVP means the proposed testnet release; later features require separate acceptance. Terms and shared states follow the [product requirements](../00-product/PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md).

## Proposed MVP

Display private descriptive measures: verified contribution count by month, active saving months and goals marked completed with evidence context. No numeric composite score, creditworthiness label, loan eligibility promise or third-party underwriting export. Contribution size does not measure character or reliability.

## Later model evaluation

Potential factors are contribution consistency, history length, completed goals and voluntary commitment fulfilment. An exact formula requires approved purpose, factor definitions, observation windows, weights, missing-data treatment, versioning, explainability, fairness and correction process. No weights or score range is silently selected.

## Acceptance / failure handling

Show last updated time and source activity; label incomplete or disputed history. Users can inspect and challenge inaccurate records. Stale/reversed data cannot feed a score as if current. Any underwriting use requires separate legal, privacy, fairness and lending review plus explicit owner decision.
