# Business model

**Status:** Draft | **Version:** 0.1 | **Last updated:** 2026-11-14  
**Responsible role:** Product owner / finance / demo lead

## Purpose

Explore revenue choices without using user funds implicitly.

## Assumptions and dependencies

This is a documentation proposal, not an implemented or approved product. The master specification and owner approval govern scope. MVP means the proposed testnet release; later features require separate acceptance. Terms and shared states follow the [product requirements](../00-product/PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md).

## Options for review

| Model | Potential value | Approval/diligence |
|---|---|---|
| Transparent service fee | Pays for useful operations | Disclosed amount/bounds, consumer/legal review, affordable unit economics |
| Optional premium tools | Additional planning/reporting | No paywall over principal exit or essential disclosures |
| Merchant-funded rewards | Partner acquisition benefit | Real contract, stock/fulfilment, consent and advertising disclosure |
| Strategy fee | Future verified strategy service | Actual yield/loss/fee accounting and legal approval; disabled MVP |
| Business partnerships | Distribution/support | Data-sharing limits, contracts and customer conflict review |

No model is approved. Proposed testnet has no platform fee and no realised revenue claim. User assets are liabilities/controlled vault funds under the selected architecture, never operating capital by default.

## Acceptance

Owner/finance choose revenue source with customer benefit, fee disclosure, cost model and jurisdiction review. Do not rely on hidden withdrawal penalties, undisclosed yield spreads, selling personal profiles or lending user money. No invented market size, customers, revenue or partner claims.
