# Fee model

**Status:** Draft | **Version:** 0.1 | **Last updated:** 2026-11-14  
**Responsible role:** Product owner / finance / demo lead

## Purpose

Make every future fee visible, bounded and legally reviewed.

## Assumptions and dependencies

This is a documentation proposal, not an implemented or approved product. The master specification and owner approval govern scope. MVP means the proposed testnet release; later features require separate acceptance. Terms and shared states follow the [product requirements](../00-product/PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md).

## Proposed testnet policy

No platform deposit/withdrawal/early-exit fee. Network execution and storage registration remain disclosed costs with an approved payer policy. Estimate source/time and possible failure fees are visible before signing. No claim that savings are free when external costs apply.

## Future pricing record

Record asset/network, fee type, integer formula/rate denominator, minimum/maximum, rounding, payer, beneficiary, effective/rule version and counsel/owner approval. Define gross request, fee and net recipient amount explicitly; user review and contract/API enforcement must agree. No fee can exceed approved bounds or appear retroactively on existing commitments.

## Acceptance and failures

Test one-unit amounts, zero balance, fee cap boundaries, provider quote changes, overflows and fees on failed actions. Legal FEE AND LIMIT POLICY placeholder remains open until values approved. Network estimate is not platform revenue; refund of unused storage is not yield. Discounts/rewards cannot conceal actual charges.

See the linked decision register; recommendations are not approvals.
