# Reward budget

**Status:** Draft | **Version:** 0.1 | **Last updated:** 2026-11-14  
**Responsible role:** Product owner / finance / demo lead

## Purpose

Require funded benefits before promising fulfilment.

## Assumptions and dependencies

This is a documentation proposal, not an implemented or approved product. The master specification and owner approval govern scope. MVP means the proposed testnet release; later features require separate acceptance. Terms and shared states follow the [product requirements](../00-product/PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md).

## Proposed MVP

Noncash points and badges create no promised monetary redemption or investment return. Proposed 10/20-point schedule and caps are unapproved; point issuance is measured separately from any future financial liability. No invented conversion rate or budget is stated.

## Later funded rewards

Before real benefits, document supplier contract, available stock, expected redemption assumptions, cost per benefit, fulfilment/refund obligation, funding source, exposure cap, approval authority and reconciliation. Merchant reimbursement risk must be separate from user principal. Stress-test all eligible users redeeming and supplier failure; do not fund benefits with undisclosed savings assets.

## Acceptance

Finance approves cost/exposure and reserve policy, counsel reviews point rights/expiry/tax implications, product publishes actual terms and operations demonstrates fulfilment/refunds. Stop new redemptions safely when funding/stock is unavailable while honouring valid existing obligations under approved terms. No marketplace activation follows automatically from approving points.
