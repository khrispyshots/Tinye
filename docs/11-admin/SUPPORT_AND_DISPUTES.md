# Support and disputes

**Status:** Draft | **Version:** 0.1 | **Last updated:** 2026-11-14  
**Responsible role:** Operations / security lead

## Purpose

Help users resolve errors without bypassing financial rules.

## Assumptions and dependencies

This is a documentation proposal, not an implemented or approved product. The master specification and owner approval govern scope. MVP means the proposed testnet release; later features require separate acceptance. Terms and shared states follow the [product requirements](../00-product/PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md).

## Triage flow

Collect category, operation reference and safe description; warn never to send keys/seed phrases. Authenticate before exposing private records. Classify wallet/access, pending/unknown, wrong asset, fee, reward, privacy, group or security issues. Preserve evidence, assign responsible role, explain current status and provide a case reference.

Financial cases compare intent, final receipt/economic effect, reservation and projection. Do not promise a blockchain reversal or guarantee recovery of an unsupported direct transfer. Reconciler may repair a proven read-model error using audited replay; cannot mint on-chain value. Reward appeals use linked correction entries.

## Acceptance

Users receive acknowledged, investigating, needs-information and resolved outcomes with reasons; response deadlines and real contacts remain legal/operations placeholders. Escalate suspected loss/security incidents promptly under the incident plan. Provide accessible dispute channels and external complaint routes after jurisdiction confirmation. Test impostor requests, unauthorised family/group organiser, unresolved payout and deleted account cases.
