# Brand asset guidelines

**Status:** Draft | **Version:** 0.1 | **Last updated:** 2026-11-14  
**Responsible role:** Brand operations

## Purpose

Govern future asset creation, licensing and exports.

## Assumptions and dependencies

This is a documentation proposal, not an implemented or approved product. The master specification and owner approval govern scope. MVP means the proposed testnet release; later features require separate acceptance. Terms and shared states follow the [product requirements](../00-product/PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md).

## Asset contract

Future assets belong in a versioned asset catalogue with identifier, author, licence, source, export dimensions, colour variant and approval record. No files or licensing claims are invented here. SVG exports must have an accessible title where meaningful, no scripts, remote references or embedded tracking. PNG exports preserve transparency and are optimised for low bandwidth.

## Usage rules

Use approved wordmark proportions and clear space from LOGO_DIRECTION. Choose forest on cream/white, or approved reversed white on forest. Never stretch, rotate, add gradients/shadows, recolour individual letters, place on busy imagery or substitute an unapproved font. A small icon must derive from the selected letterforms.

## Acceptance and edge cases

Verify favicon legibility in browser light/dark chrome, app icon mask crops, print monochrome and high-density raster exports. Include file checksums, preview sheet and licence notices at delivery. Missing font rights or cultural validation blocks publication. Final asset generation is a later approved design task; this document is its brief.

See the linked decision register; recommendations are not approvals.
