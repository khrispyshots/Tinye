# Logo direction

**Status:** Draft | **Version:** 0.1 | **Last updated:** 2026-11-14  
**Responsible role:** Brand designer

## Purpose

Specify considered wordmark exploration and future exports.

## Assumptions and dependencies

This is a documentation proposal, not an implemented or approved product. The master specification and owner approval govern scope. MVP means the proposed testnet release; later features require separate acceptance. Terms and shared states follow the [product requirements](../00-product/PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md).

## Letterform concepts for review

| Concept | Construction | Rationale / risk |
|---|---|---|
| Quiet insertion | Custom lowercase t with short crossbar and open e aperture | Suggests placing something deliberately; avoid making t resemble a cross icon |
| Shared rhythm | Related t/i stems, restrained y descender, generous n counter | Consistency without a generic geometric-font wordmark; test at small size |
| Compact ti mark | Purpose-built t/i ligature derived from approved wordmark | App recognisability; must remain readable without a coin/vault metaphor |

Explore black silhouettes first, then forest and reversed white. Draw custom curves, spacing and joins; do not label default-font text a final logo. Compare at 16/24/32-pixel icon sizes and 72-pixel wordmark width. Proposed clear space: one lowercase i stem height; proposed minimum width 72px digital and 18mm print, subject to proof testing.

## Acceptance / deliverables after approval

Owner selects direction after optical-size, one-colour, light/dark and distinctive-shape review. Deliver clean SVG outlines, transparent PNG at 1x/2x/3x, favicon 16/32, app icons 180/192/512 and source vectors. No final logo or exports have been generated in this documentation phase. Do not add piggy banks, currency signs, vault doors or growth arrows.
