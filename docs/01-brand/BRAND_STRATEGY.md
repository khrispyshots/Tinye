# Brand strategy

**Status:** Draft | **Version:** 0.1 | **Last updated:** 2026-11-14  
**Responsible role:** Brand lead

## Purpose

Position Tinye as purposeful, understandable savings.

## Assumptions and dependencies

This is a documentation proposal, not an implemented or approved product. The master specification and owner approval govern scope. MVP means the proposed testnet release; later features require separate acceptance. Terms and shared states follow the [product requirements](../00-product/PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md).

## Positioning

For people who struggle to set aside money consistently, Tinye organises contributions around personal goals and explains verified progress. Premium means restrained design, careful financial language and dependable behaviour. Approachability means ordinary words, useful errors and understandable control of funds.

Primary line: **Put something aside. Build something bigger.** Supporting lines: Save with purpose; Small steps. Meaningful progress; Make room for what matters; Your goals deserve a plan. Use a single line on welcome/marketing surfaces; transaction screens prioritise facts.

The name's Igbo inspiration comes from the supplied brief. Fluent speaker review must validate wording, dialect and context before a definitive cultural explanation is published. Do not claim an exclusive translation or imply endorsement from a cultural community.

## Acceptance and risks

Test whether first-time savers understand the product without interpreting it as an insured bank account or guaranteed investment. No stock crypto imagery, exaggerated growth claims or borrowed Apple layouts. Final identity requires owner approval and clearance checks; a proposed palette is not a registered trademark.
