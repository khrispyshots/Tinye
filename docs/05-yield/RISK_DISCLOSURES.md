# Yield risk disclosures

**Status:** Draft | **Version:** 0.1 | **Last updated:** 2026-11-14  
**Responsible role:** Product / finance / engineering leads

## Purpose

Specify truthful future earnings warnings.

## Assumptions and dependencies

This is a documentation proposal, not an implemented or approved product. The master specification and owner approval govern scope. MVP means the proposed testnet release; later features require separate acceptance. Terms and shared states follow the [product requirements](../00-product/PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md).

## Disclosure requirements

Before any future strategy allocation, explain possible principal loss, smart-contract exploits, counterparty failure, asset volatility/depeg, variable rates, network fees, liquidity/exit queues, administrator powers, oracle failure and regulatory change. Show who controls the position and who can pause or upgrade contracts. No implication that stablecoin equals bank deposit, risk-free return or insured savings.

Rates must identify source, observation time, gross/net basis and compounding assumptions. Historical performance does not establish future returns. Projected earnings must be clearly separate from realised, available funds. Fees and loss allocation need full-page review and versioned acceptance.

## MVP acceptance

Yield surfaces say unavailable; no rate card or simulated earnings appears alongside verified savings. Legal YIELD_AND_EARNINGS_DISCLOSURE remains a conditional draft. Counsel must assess the actual provider and jurisdiction before any public investment language. If strategy risk cannot be explained in clear language or exit information is missing, disable allocation.
