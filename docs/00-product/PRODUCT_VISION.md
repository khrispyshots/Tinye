# Product vision

**Status:** Draft | **Version:** 0.1 | **Last updated:** 2026-11-14  
**Responsible role:** Product lead

## Purpose

Define the human outcome and product boundaries.

## Assumptions and dependencies

This is a documentation proposal, not an implemented or approved product. The master specification and owner approval govern scope. MVP means the proposed testnet release; later features require separate acceptance. Terms and shared states follow the [product requirements](PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md).

## Vision and principles

Tinye helps people put money aside for meaningful goals, maintain a saving habit and understand verified progress. **Put something aside. Build something bigger.** Save is the core pillar; Grow requires an approved real strategy; Reward recognises eligible behaviour; Build describes savings history; Together is an optional shared-goal experience.

The experience must explain control of funds, fees and access before a contribution. Calm language, mobile access and low-bandwidth resilience take priority over crypto terminology. Principal protection is a design priority, never a guarantee of safety or insurance.

## Intended outcomes

A first-time saver understands a goal, creates it, contributes test assets, sees confirmed progress and withdraws under the disclosed rules. An experienced wallet user can inspect asset identity, network, receipts and contract addresses without forcing these details on everyone.

## Acceptance and failure boundaries

A user can explain who holds keys and who controls deposited funds. All balances distinguish confirmed, reserved and stale values. No growth or reward claim appears without evidence. A beautiful dashboard with simulated activity does not satisfy the vision. Lending, underwriting, automatic debits and real-money launch are outside this proposal.
