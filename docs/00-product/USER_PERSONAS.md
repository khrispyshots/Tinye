# Research personas and jobs

**Status:** Draft | **Version:** 0.1 | **Last updated:** 2026-11-14  
**Responsible role:** UX researcher

## Purpose

Describe hypotheses to validate without inventing interviews or demographics.

## Assumptions and dependencies

This is a documentation proposal, not an implemented or approved product. The master specification and owner approval govern scope. MVP means the proposed testnet release; later features require separate acceptance. Terms and shared states follow the [product requirements](PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md).

## Persona hypotheses

| Persona | Job to be done | Barrier | Required accommodation |
|---|---|---|---|
| First-time saver | Start an emergency goal with a small contribution | Unclear rules and fear of loss | Plain amount entry, terms summary, no minimum invented |
| Life-event planner | Track rent, education or equipment progress | Irregular income and changing date | Editable target/date, no shame for gaps |
| Consistent saver | Review contributions over time | Fragmented records | Searchable verified history and export |
| Wallet-familiar user | Earmark an approved asset | Wrong token/network and opaque fees | Contract details and exact units on review |
| Blockchain-unfamiliar user | Save without learning protocol jargon | Key loss, signing and gas confusion | Wallet explanation, recovery warnings, help before connect |
| Friends/family organiser | Share progress without losing ownership | Social pressure and pooled authority | Optional later membership, granular visibility, no admin spending |

These are design hypotheses, not validated market research. Include screen-reader users, people with low vision, motor impairments, shared phones, intermittent connectivity and limited data in recruitment. Do not equate balance size with responsibility or exclude irregular earners from recognition.

## Research protocol and acceptance

After approval, test comprehension of testnet value, key control, withdrawal rights, pending states and reward meaning using task-based interviews. Collect consent, minimise recordings and offer accessible alternatives. Record observed problems, sample limitations and design changes. Do not publish conversion targets or persona prevalence without data.
