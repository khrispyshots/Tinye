# Legal document versioning

**Status:** Draft | **Version:** 0.1 | **Last updated:** 2026-11-14  
**Responsible role:** Qualified counsel / privacy lead / product owner

## Purpose

Make acceptance traceable to immutable approved content.

## Assumptions and dependencies

This is a documentation proposal, not an implemented or approved product. The master specification and owner approval govern scope. MVP means the proposed testnet release; later features require separate acceptance. Terms and shared states follow the [product requirements](../00-product/PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md).

> **Unpublished legal draft. Not legal advice or evidence of compliance. Do not publish until counsel reviews the actual product, jurisdictions and every unresolved placeholder.**

## Version rules

Draft version 0.1 is unpublished. After counsel/owner approval assign document key, semantic version, language, effective date, content hash, approver and superseded version. Retain the exact artefact accepted by each user. Material product/fee/custody changes require a new reviewed version and notice/renewed acceptance where legally required.

Consent records store document key/version/hash, user, purpose, accepted_at UTC and channel. Marketing preference and privacy-notice acknowledgement are distinct from contractual acceptance. Never update published content in place while preserving its hash/version. A request using a superseded version must receive the current content for review if acceptance is required.

## Acceptance

Publication pipeline rejects unresolved `TO BE CONFIRMED` markers and missing approval evidence. Test stale form submission, changed locale, withdrawn optional consent, policy correction and audit retrieval. An unapproved draft is never presented as a binding production agreement. Counsel determines effective-date/notice periods, not this generator.
