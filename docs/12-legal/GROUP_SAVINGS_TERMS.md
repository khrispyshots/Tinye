# Group savings terms — conditional draft

**Status:** Draft | **Version:** 0.1 | **Last updated:** 2026-11-14  
**Responsible role:** Qualified counsel / privacy lead / product owner

## Purpose

Preserve individual ownership if groups are later approved.

## Assumptions and dependencies

This is a documentation proposal, not an implemented or approved product. The master specification and owner approval govern scope. MVP means the proposed testnet release; later features require separate acceptance. Terms and shared states follow the [product requirements](../00-product/PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md).

> **Unpublished legal draft. Not legal advice or evidence of compliance. Do not publish until counsel reviews the actual product, jurisdictions and every unresolved placeholder.**

## Not enabled in MVP

Groups are deferred under D12. These terms are not operative and do not create a pooled savings or rotating payout arrangement.

## Proposed later terms

Members explicitly accept invitation, visibility and group rule versions. Organisers manage shared metadata/invitations only; each member retains their own attributed balance and signs their own withdrawals. Organisers cannot spend, seize, reassign or pledge another member's funds. Group targets and social roles do not alter individual withdrawal terms.

Explain who sees contributions, member departure/removal, failed/late contributions, dispute process and closure. Departure must preserve eligible exit; no social penalty or principal forfeiture. Group closure may stop new contributions but cannot erase members' claims. Public-chain records may expose transactions even when the app view is private.

## Acceptance

Complete [GROUP RULES — TO BE CONFIRMED] and [CUSTODY AND CONTROL DISCLOSURE — TO BE CONFIRMED]; review privacy, governance and jurisdiction. Any pooled/rotating model needs a separate legal and technical design. Disputes do not grant unilateral organiser withdrawal authority.
