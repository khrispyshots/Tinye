# Third-party processor register

**Status:** Draft | **Version:** 0.1 | **Last updated:** 2026-11-14  
**Responsible role:** Qualified counsel / privacy lead / product owner

## Purpose

Inventory proposed vendors and block unsupported partner claims.

## Assumptions and dependencies

This is a documentation proposal, not an implemented or approved product. The master specification and owner approval govern scope. MVP means the proposed testnet release; later features require separate acceptance. Terms and shared states follow the [product requirements](../00-product/PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md).

> **Unpublished legal draft. Not legal advice or evidence of compliance. Do not publish until counsel reviews the actual product, jurisdictions and every unresolved placeholder.**

## Register — none selected

| Capability | Proposed data | Required diligence / contractual record | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hosting/database/backups | Private profile, consent, operational evidence | Entity, regions, DPA, subprocessors, encryption, export/deletion, incident terms | Unselected |
| Authentication | Identifiers, sessions and recovery factors | Key/control model, security, region, deletion, availability | Unselected |
| External wallet/connector | Public accounts, message/transaction requests | Independent controller/processor role, manifest supply chain, recovery, mobile/accessibility | NEAR Connect recommended; exact provider set/version unselected |
| RPC/indexer | Public chain requests/records, possible IP/account linkage | Logs, regions, receipt/archive access, quotas, fallback and terms | Unselected |
| Notifications | Minimum destination and template | Consent, DPA, deliverability, unsubscribe, region and retention | Unselected |
| Analytics/error tracking | Pseudonymous safe events | Redaction, consent/basis, residency, retention and opt-out | Unselected |
| Support | Case details and limited evidence | Role restriction, DPA, export/deletion and incident contacts | Unselected |
| Yield/fiat/merchant | Conditional financial or identity data | Regulatory status, due diligence, custody, fees and fulfilment | Deferred |

## Acceptance

Complete [PROCESSOR REGISTER — TO BE CONFIRMED] with actual legal entity, role, purpose, locations, transfer safeguard, contract date, subprocessors, retention, security review and owner. Do not call every third party a processor automatically; counsel determines role. A recommendation is not a vendor relationship or permission to send data.
