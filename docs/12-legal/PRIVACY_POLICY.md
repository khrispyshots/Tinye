## Data and purpose

| Data | Proposed use | Sharing / limits |
|---|---|---|
| Authentication identifiers and sessions | Secure access, recovery and abuse prevention | Selected auth/hosting processors only |
| Wallet account/public-chain references | Ownership proof, transaction verification and reconciliation | Public blockchain is independently visible |
| Private profile/goal metadata | Personal goal experience and support | Owner-only by default; minimum staff access |
| Financial operation evidence | Verified history, disputes and accountability | Restricted verifier/support roles |
| Device/technical logs | Reliability and security | Minimise identifiers; no secret payloads |
| Support communications | Resolve requested cases | Assigned support and approved processor |
| Consent/preferences | Demonstrate agreed versions and honour optional choices | Restricted legal/operational access |
| Behavioural summaries | Private saving-history reflection | No credit scoring or partner underwriting in MVP |

## Processors, transfers and retention

Providers, regions, contracts and safeguards appear in the processor register; none is selected. International transfers require approved safeguards and jurisdiction analysis. Retention periods are [RETENTION SCHEDULE — TO BE CONFIRMED]; legal holds are documented, reviewed and minimised. Deleting private data cannot erase immutable/public chain records; avoid placing private purpose information on-chain.

## Rights, analytics and security

Users can request access, correction, export, deletion and other rights applicable under law through [PRIVACY CONTACT — TO BE CONFIRMED]. Verify identity proportionately; never request seed phrases. Essential sessions differ from optional analytics/marketing; counsel decides consent requirements. Proposed analytics excludes direct identifiers and wallet addresses. No consequential automated decisions or underwriting score is enabled.

Security is designed around encryption, access control, minimisation, audit and incident response; no claim of perfect safety. Breach notification is assessed against applicable law and facts. Children/age restrictions and parental handling remain unresolved. Policy changes are versioned with appropriate notice. External complaint authority and process are [PRIVACY COMPLAINT ROUTE — TO BE CONFIRMED].
