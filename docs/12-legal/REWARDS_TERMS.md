## Proposed rules

Points are nontransferable loyalty units with no cash value, no withdrawal right and no credit/lending entitlement. The proposed schedule is 10 points for the first eligible confirmed goal deposit and 20 for the first valid target-reaching event, each limited to three earning goals per user per calendar month. These values, the qualifying contribution floor, caps and no-expiry policy are **unapproved** and cannot be activated until D13/D20 are resolved.

Only verified eligible activity counts; pending/failed/refunded/unattributed or duplicate events do not. Invalid awards may be corrected with linked ledger entries and an explanation/appeal. Ordinary withdrawal does not confiscate principal or reverse a valid earned milestone solely because the user exited. No paid chance-based rewards or misleading monetary equivalence.

## Redemption and changes

Real catalogue, partners and fulfilment are unavailable. Future redemption needs stock, eligibility, price, expiry, delivery and refund rules plus funded supplier commitments. Do not promise future point conversion. [REWARD RULES — TO BE CONFIRMED] and [SUPPORT CONTACT — TO BE CONFIRMED] block publication. Material changes need notice and lawful treatment of existing benefits; no silent devaluation.
