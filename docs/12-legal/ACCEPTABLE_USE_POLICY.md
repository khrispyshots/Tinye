# Acceptable use policy — draft

**Status:** Draft | **Version:** 0.1 | **Last updated:** 2026-11-14  
**Responsible role:** Qualified counsel / privacy lead / product owner

## Purpose

Define fair usage restrictions without arbitrary fund control.

## Assumptions and dependencies

This is a documentation proposal, not an implemented or approved product. The master specification and owner approval govern scope. MVP means the proposed testnet release; later features require separate acceptance. Terms and shared states follow the [product requirements](../00-product/PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md).

> **Unpublished legal draft. Not legal advice or evidence of compliance. Do not publish until counsel reviews the actual product, jurisdictions and every unresolved placeholder.**

## Proposed obligations

Do not use Tinye for fraud, unlawful transfers, sanctions evasion, impersonation, exploitation of vulnerable users, harassment, spam or unauthorised system access. Do not submit malicious payloads, manipulate reward eligibility or attempt to use another user's account/wallet. Lawful security reports follow the disclosure policy and do not require access to user assets.

## Enforcement safeguards

Investigate proportionately, preserve evidence and provide reason/appeal where lawful. Off-chain restrictions can limit new account activity; they do not create an undisclosed power to take principal or rewrite contract ownership. Sanctions/AML and lawful authority processes require counsel-defined scope, identity checks and confidentiality where required.

## Acceptance

[ELIGIBILITY POLICY — TO BE CONFIRMED], [ENFORCEMENT AND AML POLICY — TO BE CONFIRMED] and verified contact details are required before publication. No blanket right to confiscate funds or change financial conditions. Test false-positive appeals, compromised accounts and protected security research before enabling enforcement workflows.
