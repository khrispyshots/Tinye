# Terms of service — draft

**Status:** Draft | **Version:** 0.1 | **Last updated:** 2026-11-14  
**Responsible role:** Qualified counsel / privacy lead / product owner

## Purpose

Provide structured user terms for counsel to complete.

## Assumptions and dependencies

This is a documentation proposal, not an implemented or approved product. The master specification and owner approval govern scope. MVP means the proposed testnet release; later features require separate acceptance. Terms and shared states follow the [product requirements](../00-product/PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md).

## Accounts and security

Provide accurate information and protect account access. Tinye will not request seed phrases/private keys. Use only approved authentication and supported wallets. Application recovery does not recover an external wallet or transfer ownership of its funds. Notify the verified support channel of suspected misuse.

## Wallets, assets and goals

Supported networks/assets and exact vault identity must be disclosed before signing. An external wallet approves actions; deposited assets follow contract rules and disclosed upgrade/emergency powers. Custody and operator control are [CUSTODY AND CONTROL DISCLOSURE — TO BE CONFIRMED]. Proposed MVP is testnet only, with test assets and no real-money savings offer. Targets, dates and reminders are planning tools, not automatic debits or promises of earnings.

## Deposits, withdrawals, fees and limits

Only verified accepted deposits count. Pending/unknown transactions are unresolved. Withdrawals require owner approval and confirmed availability less reservations, network costs and disclosed limits. Proposed testnet mode is flexible with no platform fee/early-exit penalty; final fee/limit policy is [FEE AND LIMIT POLICY — TO BE CONFIRMED]. Network fees may apply on failure. Blockchain transfers may be irreversible; support cannot promise reversal. No principal confiscation or silent restriction is authorised by these drafts.

## Yield, rewards and groups

Yield, real redemption and groups are unavailable in proposed MVP. Noncash points require approved rules, have no proposed monetary value and are separate from savings. Any future strategy, partner reward or shared-fund product requires its own reviewed terms and opt-in; no guaranteed return or credit approval claim.

## Acceptable use and third parties

Do not use the service for unlawful activity, fraud, harassment or security attacks. Wallets, RPC services and other approved vendors have separate terms and risks; provider identity is [PROVIDER DISCLOSURES — TO BE CONFIRMED]. No banking, licence, insurance or deposit-protection claim is made.

## Availability, suspension and closure

Maintenance, internet and protocol outages can delay service. Explain restrictions with reasons and appeal where lawful; restrictions must not imply arbitrary authority to take funds. Account closure requires unresolved operations and eligible balances to be addressed. Off-chain deletion follows the approved retention schedule; public-chain records remain.
