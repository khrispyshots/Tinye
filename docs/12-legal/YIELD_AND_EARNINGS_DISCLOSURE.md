# Yield and earnings disclosure — conditional draft

**Status:** Draft | **Version:** 0.1 | **Last updated:** 2026-11-14  
**Responsible role:** Qualified counsel / privacy lead / product owner

## Purpose

Keep future strategy terms separate from unavailable MVP functionality.

## Assumptions and dependencies

This is a documentation proposal, not an implemented or approved product. The master specification and owner approval govern scope. MVP means the proposed testnet release; later features require separate acceptance. Terms and shared states follow the [product requirements](../00-product/PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md).

> **Unpublished legal draft. Not legal advice or evidence of compliance. Do not publish until counsel reviews the actual product, jurisdictions and every unresolved placeholder.**

## Not applicable to enabled MVP

No yield source, strategy, rate, counterparty or earnings distribution is approved. This required document exists as a conditional structured draft; it must not imply that Tinye offers an investment or interest-bearing account.

## Required future schedule

Before activation, complete [YIELD STRATEGY DISCLOSURE — TO BE CONFIRMED] covering legal provider, verified contracts, asset/network, custody, strategy mechanics, administrator/upgrader powers, fees, liquidity/exit queue, loss allocation and emergency exit. Publish rate source/time, gross/net basis, APR/APY definition and compounding assumptions. Distinguish estimated accrual from realised and withdrawable earnings.

## User warnings and acceptance

Rates vary; historical rates do not promise future performance. Principal may be lost and exits delayed. Stablecoin, protocol, counterparty and regulatory risks require strategy-specific language. No guarantee, insurance or fixed return is stated. Counsel and owner must approve economics, classification and user consent; technical reconciliation must prove attribution before any earnings display. D07/D08 remain open.
