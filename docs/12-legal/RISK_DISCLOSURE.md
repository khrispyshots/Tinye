## Proposed testnet notice

Tinye is not currently a deployed financial service. A future testnet demonstration uses test assets and must not be described as insured savings, a bank deposit or withdrawable real earnings. Public testnet data may reset.

## Risks for counsel to adapt

Contract vulnerabilities can cause inaccessible or lost assets. External wallets can be compromised or keys lost; application recovery does not recover keys. Network congestion, fees, RPC/indexer outages and asynchronous execution can delay or obscure status. Submitted transactions may be irreversible. Assets may fluctuate; stablecoins may depeg or carry issuer/freeze risk. Upgrader/emergency authorities may affect contract behaviour and require precise disclosure.

Third-party custody/yield, liquidity queues, variable return/loss and group disputes are conditional risks only if those features are approved; do not imply integrations exist. Regulatory changes, phishing, service outages and reward availability also need clear explanations. No insurance, protection scheme, guaranteed return or recovery capability is claimed.
