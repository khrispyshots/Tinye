# Presentation outline

**Status:** Draft | **Version:** 0.1 | **Last updated:** 2026-11-14  
**Responsible role:** Product owner / finance / demo lead

## Purpose

Structure an honest concise narrative for later presentation.

## Assumptions and dependencies

This is a documentation proposal, not an implemented or approved product. The master specification and owner approval govern scope. MVP means the proposed testnet release; later features require separate acceptance. Terms and shared states follow the [product requirements](../00-product/PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md).

## Proposed narrative

1. Human need: saving with purpose and confidence; identify assumptions rather than invented statistics.
2. Tinye promise: small consistent actions, verified progress and clear access rules.
3. Journey: onboarding, goal, reviewed contribution, final confirmation, inspected history and eligible exit.
4. Why NEAR: conditional native-network vault and user signing; show actual integration evidence only after it exists.
5. Architecture/trust: private metadata off-chain, enforceable ownership on-chain, operator/upgrader powers and recovery limits.
6. Rewards/history: noncash milestones and descriptive activity, no credit/return promise.
7. Evidence/limitations: current stage, tests, security/legal status and disabled advanced features.
8. Roadmap/request: documentation decisions, then approved testnet implementation; mainnet remains separate.

## Acceptance

No slide deck is created in this documentation task. Keep claims consistent with FEATURES_AND_LIMITATIONS and update after accepted phases. Any screenshots or recorded financial activity must show environment and source date. Do not imply a logo exploration or architecture diagram is a shipped application.
