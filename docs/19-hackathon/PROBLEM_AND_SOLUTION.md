# Problem and solution

**Status:** Draft | **Version:** 0.1 | **Last updated:** 2026-11-14  
**Responsible role:** Product owner / finance / demo lead

## Purpose

Explain user need without inventing traction or research.

## Assumptions and dependencies

This is a documentation proposal, not an implemented or approved product. The master specification and owner approval govern scope. MVP means the proposed testnet release; later features require separate acceptance. Terms and shared states follow the [product requirements](../00-product/PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md).

## Problem hypothesis

People may struggle to save consistently when money is not linked to an understandable goal, progress is fragmented and financial conditions are unclear. The supplied brief motivates this hypothesis; no interviews, survey, market-size estimate or conversion data has been collected.

## Proposed solution

A simple goal journey, exact contribution/exit reviews, verified history, private progress and clearly explained noncash milestones. Blockchain is supporting infrastructure: users should understand funds control and actions without learning protocol terminology. No yield is promised; advanced products are deferred.

## Validation and acceptance

After approval, test whether target users can explain testnet value, key control, contract risk, pending vs confirmed and exit rules. Measure baseline onboarding/first deposit/retention and qualitative confusion; do not invent targets. A successful solution must preserve financial accuracy and user agency under failure, not just improve conversion. Research consent, accessible recruitment and data minimisation are required.
