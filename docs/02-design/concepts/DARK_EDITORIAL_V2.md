# Dark editorial concept — revision 2

**Status:** Draft visual exploration | **Version:** 0.2 | **Last updated:** 2026-11-14

## Purpose

Respond to owner feedback: retain dark mode while removing generic visual treatment from concept 03.

## Design changes

Warm carbon and olive-grey surfaces, cream actions, oversized financial numerals with smaller unit labels, ruled goal rows, numbered goals and segmented progress replace repeated panels, mint buttons, donut charts and botanical decoration. The former dark concept remains preserved.
