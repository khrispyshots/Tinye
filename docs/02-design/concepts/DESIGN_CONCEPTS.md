### Concept 1

Use case: ui-mockup. Create a high-fidelity app design concept presentation for Tinye, a goal-based savings app. Concept title "01 / Quiet Forest". Landscape board with two large front-facing mobile app screens, left Home dashboard and right personal goal detail, no perspective tilt, maximally legible crisp interface. Off-white #F8F9F6 background, white cards, forest #174D38 actions, ink #202923 text, sage #E5F0E8 accents. Geist-like modern sans, spacious precise hierarchy, subtle dividers, restrained 20px corners. Original Apple-like restraint and premium fintech clarity, not a copy of an existing app. Lowercase typographic tinye as provisional wordmark. Home: small "Design preview • sample data" banner, clear "Testnet" badge, "Make room for what matters.", confirmed savings "1,250 TEST", supporting "Test assets only", large Add to goal and outlined Withdraw buttons, section Your goals with Emergency fund 750 / 1,000 TEST progress 75%, New laptop 500 / 2,000 TEST progress 25%. Goal detail: Emergency fund, elegant linear progress 75%, 750 TEST saved of 1,000 TEST, available 750 TEST, reserved 0 TEST, Flexible savings, no lock-in, Review withdrawal terms, Add to goal and activity link. No fabricated transaction list. Bottom navigation Home, Goals, Save, Rewards, Profile with refined outline icons. Under phones one short descriptor "Calm. Clear. Effortless." Small board footer "Visual concept only — not a working financial product." No yield, APR, fiat values, crypto coins, glassmorphism, gradients, or invented real financial activity. Screen content must show sample labels. polished professional design presentation with aligned margins.
