# Experimentation policy

**Status:** Draft | **Version:** 0.1 | **Last updated:** 2026-11-14  
**Responsible role:** Security / operations / privacy leads

## Purpose

Protect informed financial choices during product testing.

## Assumptions and dependencies

This is a documentation proposal, not an implemented or approved product. The master specification and owner approval govern scope. MVP means the proposed testnet release; later features require separate acceptance. Terms and shared states follow the [product requirements](../00-product/PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md).

## Allowed experiments

After approval, test educational copy, navigation and nonfinancial layout with a written hypothesis, eligible cohort, privacy basis, primary metric and guardrails. Exclude users who cannot give valid consent or are outside approved scope. Document assignment/version and stop conditions; avoid unnecessary tracking.

## Prohibited without separate review

No hidden changes to custody, access, fees, withdrawal timing, committed rules, rewards entitlement, risk wording, yield claims or required consent. Do not optimise deposits through shame, urgency or misleading balances. Never randomise users into principal penalties or suppress failure/error disclosures.

## Acceptance

Product, security and counsel review experiments affecting financial comprehension or processing. Report sample limits, missing data and harms alongside conversion. Stop on increased confusion, complaints, unauthorised behaviour or financial discrepancies. Financial truth and accessibility are guardrails, not variant choices. No experiment is active in this documentation phase.

## Unresolved decisions

See the linked decision register; recommendations are not approvals.
