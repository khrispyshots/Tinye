# Group savings

**Status:** Draft | **Version:** 0.1 | **Last updated:** 2026-11-14  
**Responsible role:** Product / finance / engineering leads

## Purpose

Scope a conditional shared-goal experience.

## Assumptions and dependencies

This is a documentation proposal, not an implemented or approved product. The master specification and owner approval govern scope. MVP means the proposed testnet release; later features require separate acceptance. Terms and shared states follow the [product requirements](../00-product/PRODUCT_REQUIREMENTS.md).

## MVP disposition and later proposal

Group savings is deferred. Later groups provide a shared progress view with individually attributable vault balances, not pooled withdrawal authority. Each member owns and signs for their own funds; an organiser manages invitations and shared metadata only.

Group creation requires a name, asset/network, target, visibility policy and rules version. Member consent specifies whether others can see identity, individual amount or aggregate progress. No public member list or contribution amount by default. Joining cannot silently change existing personal-goal rules.

## Acceptance / failures

A member can leave without giving another member control or forfeiting principal. A group may close to new contributions while exits remain available. Late/failed contributions create neutral status, no shame or financial penalty. Disputes route to support with evidence; no organiser adjudication over funds. Any pooled model needs a new custody, governance and legal specification.
